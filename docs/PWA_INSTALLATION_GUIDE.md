# 📱 How to Install SAGALA SERVICE as a Mobile App (PWA)

SAGALA SERVICE is built as a **Progressive Web App (PWA)**. It installs directly on Android phones and Apple iPhones from the browser — **with zero App Store or Play Store downloads, zero fees, and no phone storage bloat**.

---

## 🤖 Installing on Android (Google Chrome / Brave / Edge)

1. Open your Chrome browser on your Android phone.
2. Visit your SAGALA SERVICE website link (e.g. `https://sagala-service.pages.dev`).
3. You will see an **"Install SAGALA App"** blue banner at the top of the feed.
   - Simply tap **"Install"**.
4. *Alternative method*:
   - Tap the **three vertical dots (⋮)** in the top right corner of Chrome.
   - Tap **"Add to Home screen"** or **"Install app"**.
   - Tap **Install**.
5. The SAGALA shield icon will now appear on your phone's home screen alongside your native apps. When opened, it launches in fullscreen mode with no browser URL bar!

---

## 🍏 Installing on iPhone / iPad (Safari)

Apple iOS requires installing through the Safari browser:

1. Open **Safari** on your iPhone or iPad.
2. Navigate to your SAGALA SERVICE URL.
3. Tap the **Share icon** at the bottom of the screen (the square box with an arrow pointing upward: ⎋ / [↑]).
4. Scroll down the share sheet and tap **"Add to Home Screen"** (icon with a plus sign: ⊞).
5. In the top right corner, tap **"Add"**.
6. The SAGALA SERVICE app icon is now pinned to your iPhone Home Screen. It launches like a native iOS app with smooth gestures!

## ⚡ Offline Functionality

Thanks to the built-in Service Worker (`dist/sw.js` via Workbox):
- The latest offers feed and visited shop profiles are cached in local memory.
- If a shopper loses connectivity in a busy Madurai bazaar, the app opens instantly without showing a connection error screen.
