# 🏪 Madurai Merchant 1-Page Onboarding Guide

> **SAGALA SERVICE: Welcome to Madurai's #1 Local Offers Network**  
> Reach thousands of active shoppers in your neighborhood without spending a single rupee on ads or commissions.

---

## 3 Easy Steps to Get Started

### Step 1: Register Your Shop (1 Minute)
1. Open the app on your phone and tap **Profile** → **Register Your Shop Now** (or select **Merchant** in the top bar).
2. Enter your shop details:
   - **Shop Name**: e.g., *Pandian Home Appliances*
   - **Category & Locality**: e.g., *Home Appliances*, *KK Nagar*
   - **Shop Address & Timings**: e.g., *78, Rajaji Street, KK Nagar Arch (9:00 AM - 9:00 PM)*
   - **Call & WhatsApp Number**: Enter your customer counter contact numbers
   - **Shop Photo / Logo**: Pick a photo from your gallery (auto-compressed in seconds)
3. Submit registration. Your shop status will display as **Pending** while the SAGALA verification team validates your location.

---

### Step 2: Post Your Flash Offers in 30 Seconds
Whenever you have fresh stock, seasonal discounts, or clearance deals:
1. Open your **Merchant Dashboard** and tap **"+ New Offer (30s)"**.
2. Type a short, catchy title:
   - *Example: "Festival Special: 55 inch 4K Smart TV at Flat 28% Off!"*
3. Enter your **Old Price** (e.g. ₹59,990) and **Offer Price** (e.g. ₹42,990). The app automatically calculates the **28% OFF** discount badge!
4. Choose duration: **24 Hours**, **3 Days**, or **7 Days**. (Expired offers auto-hide so customers only see live deals).
5. Tap **"🚀 Publish Offer Live"**.
6. The app instantly generates a unique 4-digit coupon code (e.g., **`SGL-3341`**) displayed to all shoppers.

---

### Step 3: Convert Calls & Mark Confirmed Sales (The "Sales Boost" Engine)

1. **Direct Leads**:
   - Shoppers tap **Call** to talk to your staff, tap **WhatsApp** with pre-filled messages (*"Hi, I saw your offer on SAGALA SERVICE"*), or tap **Directions** to navigate to your storefront via Google Maps.
2. **At Your Billing Counter**:
   - The customer mentions their coupon code (e.g. `SGL-3341`) to redeem the deal.
   - In your Merchant Dashboard, tap **"Mark Sale"**, type `SGL-3341`, and enter the billing amount.
   - Tap **"Confirm Sale"**.
3. **Watch Your Sales Boost**:
   - Your dashboard tracks total impressions, direct calls, confirmed sales, and total influenced revenue.
   - Every week, your **Weekly Growth Summary** shows exactly how many leads and sales SAGALA brought to your counter!
